Pancreatic cancer cells can acquire cholesterol from their surroundings or make it themselves. The Perera Lab’s 2025 study connects these strategies to the environments in which metastases grow. But a group-level result leaves a practical question: which individual model and perturbation would be a useful starting point for the next experiment?

I built a small, reproducible review tool over the study’s colony measurements. It keeps each cell model visible, asks whether two guides agree, and checks how much a single observation can change the shortlist. This is an experimental planning aid, not a new validation of the paper’s mechanism.

How it works

The source contains six PDAC models, seven conditions and three biological replicates per condition: 126 reported colony measurements. Within each model, the mean for each knockout guide is compared with the non-targeting control (sgNT). Models are never pooled into a larger apparent sample size.

The two-guide check uses the smaller reduction. A sensitivity check then removes one observation at a time from either guide or the control. A candidate stays on the shortlist only if it meets your chosen reduction threshold in every scenario. The threshold is a planning choice, not a significance test.

A concrete next experiment

Before committing to a mechanism experiment, confirm the guide edits and target-protein depletion, repeat the colony assay with new independent cultures, and retain sgNT plus both guides. A rescue or complementary perturbation could then help separate on-target dependence from guide-specific effects. These are proposed checks, not experiments performed here.

Methods note

Reduction = 100 × (1 − mean guide / mean sgNT). The concordant effect is the minimum of the two guide reductions. Sensitivity removes each of nine relevant observations once, recalculates the group means, and retains the smallest concordant effect across these scenarios and the full dataset. It is not a confidence interval, a formal outlier rule or a statistical test. No sample is permanently excluded. With only three biological replicates per condition, sensitivity checks cannot establish reproducibility.

The published model groups are C2-Lung (CAPAN-2, HPAC, HPAF-II) and C1-Liver (MiaPaCa-2, KP4, Panc-1). They describe the study’s model classification, not patient metastatic risk. Colony growth alone does not measure cholesterol flux, organ colonization or clinical response. Source processing and normalization are not reconstructed from raw colony images.

Source: Rademaker et al. (2025), Fig. 3c,d and legend, source workbook. Published measurements are attributed to their authors; the thresholds, sensitivity analysis and review interface are this project’s additions.
